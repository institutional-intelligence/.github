# Institutional Intelligence

**Ideas matter. Institutions determine whether they survive.**

Institutional Intelligence is an open project devoted to understanding how ideas, values, communities, creative projects, and social systems become durable structures capable of surviving beyond the enthusiasm—or lifetime—of their founders.

We study the difference between having an idea and building something that can actually carry it.

## What is Institutional Intelligence?

Institutional intelligence is the ability to recognize, design, navigate, maintain, and improve the structures through which people coordinate over time.

That includes things such as:

- governance
- roles and responsibilities
- rules and procedures
- incentives
- succession
- ownership
- dispute resolution
- resource management
- documentation
- adaptation and versioning

A useful shorthand is:

> **If an idea tells you what matters, institutional intelligence asks: “What makes it keep working?”**

## What You'll Find Here

This organization is a home for open documents, frameworks, protocols, and experiments in institutional design.

Projects may range from small creative collaborations and voluntary associations to cultural, philosophical, and community institutions.

Some documents are intended not merely to be read, but to be **used, modified, tested, and forked**.

## The Laboratory

This is not intended to be the final word on institutional design.

It's a workshop.

Fork things. Modify them. Test them. Find failure modes. Build better versions.

**Turn ideas into institutions.**
